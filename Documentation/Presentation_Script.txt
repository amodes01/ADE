What is Polarization (1 min)
-	Arxika prepei na milisoume gia to ti einai Polarization, isxuei otan yparxei ena megalo divide stin stasi ton anthropon i kapoiwn groups, otan oi apopseis tous
ginontai olo kai perissotero antithetes, xoris ena middle ground.
 Gia afti tin Diplomatiki mas endiaferei perissotero, to ideologiko polarization kai to affective Polarization. Diladi otan iparxoun 2 extreme opinions pou kanoun teleios contrast se ena
topic, kathos kai episis otan iparxei polarization metaksi anthropon, when they just dislike the people who hold the opinion. Afto odigei se atoma na akoune mono opinions me
paromio stance mazi tous, me apotelesma na ginete spread misinformation.


Real World Examples of Polarization (1-2 min)
- 	Kapoia Paradeigmata apo Polarization Recently einai ta US elections 2024, ta France Olympics 2024 kathos kai to Brexit 2016 to opoio tha doume pio leptomeros meta,
Ta US elections einai ena prime candidate gia Polarization, opou ta parties pou itan polarized itan oi Democrats me tous Republicans gia ta Candidates tous, ipirxe poli
distrust kai misinformation kathos kai polli affective polarization, opou polloi voters kanan vote gia to allo party apla epidi kanan dislike to candidate.
France Olympics itan kai afta ena poli polarizing topic logo 2 kirious logous, ena itan to depiction tou last supper pou epidiksan pou eixe megalo backlash worldwide, kathos
kai to topic tis Gold Medalist sto Women's Boxing Imane Khelif, to opoio ekane spur ena controversy wave gia to gender tis.
Telefteo einai to topic tou brexit, to opoio einai ena studied kai textbook definition tou Political Polarization, kathos apofasisan na figei i Aglia apo tin Evropaiki Enosi,
To opoio ekane polarize ton kosmo se Leave I Remain, globalists vs nationalists, to opoio epirease olokliro ton kosmo para mono tin Aglia.
